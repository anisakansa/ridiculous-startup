## The pitch (133 characters)

> We rent out trained goats to mow your lawn, fertilize it, and leave — the oldest landscaping crew on earth, now bookable by the hour.

The turn is the whole joke: the reader laughs at "rent out trained goats", then nods at the second half — the goats mow, fertilise, and leave, and the booking model (by the hour) is how a real landscaping crew already charges. Goat grazing crews are a real service; the sentence makes the market obvious without explaining it.
